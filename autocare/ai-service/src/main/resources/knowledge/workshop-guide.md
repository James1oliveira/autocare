# AutoCare workshop guide

Each section describes common symptoms, the usual causes and which service fixes it.
The assistant searches these notes to answer customers (retrieval-augmented generation).

## Brakes grinding, squealing or vibrating
Service code: BRAKE_SERVICE
Symptoms: grinding or metal-on-metal noise when braking, high-pitched squeal, steering wheel or brake pedal shakes or vibrates when slowing down from high speed, car takes longer to stop, brake warning light.
Likely causes: brake pads worn down to the backing plate, warped or worn brake discs, low brake fluid.
Advice: worn brakes are a safety risk. Avoid hard braking and book an inspection as soon as possible. If the pedal goes soft or to the floor, do not drive the car.

## Check engine light or warning light on
Service code: DIAGNOSTIC_SCAN
Symptoms: check engine light or engine management light on, car feels down on power, limp mode, rough idle, higher fuel consumption, other dashboard warning lights.
Likely causes: faulty sensor (oxygen, MAF, MAP), ignition coil or spark plug problem, boost leak on turbo engines, EGR or DPF issues on diesels.
Advice: a flashing check engine light means a misfire, so stop driving and have the car towed. A steady light is usually safe for short trips until the scan.

## Engine overheating or losing coolant
Service code: COOLING_SYSTEM
Symptoms: temperature gauge rising into the red, steam from the bonnet, sweet smell, coolant level keeps dropping, puddles of green, pink or orange fluid under the car, heater blowing cold.
Likely causes: coolant leak from a hose or radiator, failed water pump, stuck thermostat, broken radiator fan.
Advice: stop driving immediately if the engine overheats, because it can warp the cylinder head. Never open the radiator cap while the engine is hot.

## Car won't start, clicking or slow cranking
Service code: BATTERY_REPLACEMENT
Symptoms: clicking sound when turning the key, engine cranks slowly, dashboard lights dim, battery warning light, car needs a jump start, problems after load shedding or cold mornings.
Likely causes: old or flat battery (most last 3 to 5 years), corroded terminals, faulty alternator not charging the battery.
Advice: a battery and charging test takes a few minutes and tells you if it's the battery or the alternator.

## Aircon blowing warm air
Service code: AIRCON_REGAS
Symptoms: aircon only blows warm or slightly cool air, weak airflow, bad smell from the vents, aircon works on and off.
Likely causes: low refrigerant from a small leak, faulty compressor clutch, blocked cabin filter.
Advice: a regas with a leak test is the usual first step before summer.

## Car pulls to one side or tyres wear unevenly
Service code: WHEEL_ALIGNMENT
Symptoms: car pulls left or right, steering wheel not straight when driving straight, inner or outer edge of tyres wearing faster, vibration at certain speeds.
Likely causes: wheel alignment knocked out by a pothole or kerb, unbalanced wheels, uneven tyre pressure.
Advice: check tyre pressures first. Alignment and balancing protects your tyres and is cheap compared to new tyres.

## Bald, damaged or punctured tyres
Service code: TYRE_REPLACEMENT
Symptoms: tread worn close to the wear bars (1.6 mm legal limit), bulge or cut on the sidewall, slow puncture, car slides in the rain.
Likely causes: normal wear, pothole damage, driving on low pressure.
Advice: a sidewall bulge can burst at speed, so replace that tyre before driving far.

## Clutch slipping or hard to change gears
Service code: CLUTCH_REPLACEMENT
Symptoms: engine revs rise but the car doesn't speed up (especially uphill), clutch pedal bites very high, burning smell, judder when pulling away, grinding when changing gears.
Likely causes: worn clutch plate, weak pressure plate, failing release bearing or hydraulic leak.
Advice: a slipping clutch gets worse quickly. Avoid towing or heavy loads until it's replaced.

## Knocking or clunking over bumps
Service code: SUSPENSION_REPAIR
Symptoms: knocking or clunking over speed bumps and potholes, car bounces after bumps, leans in corners, uneven tyre wear, steering feels loose.
Likely causes: worn shock absorbers, broken stabiliser links, worn control arm bushes or ball joints.
Advice: worn suspension makes braking and handling worse, so have it inspected soon.

## Regular service due
Service code: MAJOR_SERVICE
Symptoms: service light on, service interval reached (often every 15,000 km or 12 months), 60,000 km or 90,000 km milestone, buying a used car with no service history.
Likely causes: routine maintenance is due.
Advice: a major service replaces all filters and checks the whole car. For an oil and filter change only, a minor service is enough.

## Oil change or engine ticking noise
Service code: MINOR_SERVICE
Symptoms: oil change due, low oil level, oil warning light, light ticking or tapping noise from the engine at idle, dark dirty oil on the dipstick.
Likely causes: old or low engine oil, worn hydraulic lifters needing fresh oil.
Advice: check the oil level right away. If the oil warning light stays on, stop the engine to avoid damage. A ticking that continues after an oil change needs a diagnostic scan.

## More power and performance tuning
Service code: PERFORMANCE_TUNE
Symptoms: wants more power or torque, Stage 1 or Stage 2 remap, turbo upgrade, downpipe, intake, exhaust or intercooler upgrade, track day preparation, dyno run.
Likely causes: not a fault, this is an upgrade request.
Advice: tuning should start with a healthy car, so a service and diagnostic check are recommended first. Some upgrades can affect warranty and roadworthiness.
